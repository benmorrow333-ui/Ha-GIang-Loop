Why book with Sailsfly?

We were tourists here once, too.

Sailsfly started with a backpacker who came to Hà Giang and never really left. We have now lived in Vietnam for over two years, most of it on the loop, and we plan every ride around the things we wish someone had shown us on our first trip.

The spots worth stopping for

Two years on these roads means we know which viewpoints are worth the climb, where to be at sunrise, and which corners the big groups ride straight past.

Connections up and down the loop

Homestay families, easy-rider drivers and local guides we have ridden with for years. That is how our groups get the good rooms and the dinners worth staying up for.

We speak the language

Semi-fluent Vietnamese, so ordering, haggling and chatting with the families you stay with is easy, not lost in translation.
