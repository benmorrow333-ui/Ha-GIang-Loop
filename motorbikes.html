Before you ride

Licence requirements

To ride legally in Hà Giang, travellers need both of the documents below. Due to legal requirements and frequent police checks, motorbikes cannot be rented without the proper licensing.

  1. 01

    Home country licence with motorcycle certification

    Your full driving licence from home must include a motorcycle endorsement. A car-only licence is not enough.

  2. 02

    International Driving Permit (1968 convention), class A

    An IDP issued under the 1968 Vienna Convention, stamped for class A motorcycles. Permits issued under the 1949 convention are not accepted.
