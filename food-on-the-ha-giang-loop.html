What to eat on the Hà Giang Loop

Mountain food is hearty, warming and unlike anything in Hanoi. These are the dishes worth stopping for along the way.

Most of the Loop’s best food is simple and local: family kitchens, market stalls and homestay dinners. Here is what to look out for.

Dishes to try

  • Phở chua (sour phở)Cold rice noodles with roast pork, peanuts, herbs and a tangy dressing. A Hà Giang speciality and perfect for lunch.
  • Thắng cốA rich Hmong stew of horse or beef and spices, traditionally served at market days. Try it at Đồng Văn or Mèo Vạc market.
  • Bánh cuốn trứngSteamed rice rolls with an egg cooked inside, dipped in a warm broth. The classic breakfast in Hà Giang City.
  • Xôi ngũ sắcFive-colour sticky rice, dyed with leaves and flowers. Made for festivals but often found at markets.
  • Bánh tam giác mạchBuckwheat cakes, sometimes grilled, made from the pink flowers that cover the plateau in autumn.
  • Thịt trâu gác bếpSmoked, dried buffalo meat hung above the kitchen fire. Chewy, smoky and a great snack for the road.
  • Cháo ấu tẩuA bitter, medicinal rice porridge eaten in the evenings to warm up. Only eat it from a trusted cook.
  • Rượu ngô (corn wine)Strong local corn spirit, poured generously at homestay dinners. Pace yourself, especially if you are riding the next day.

Sailsfly’s tips

Eat where the locals eat, especially at the weekly markets. Ask your homestay host what’s in season. And if you are vegetarian, tell your guide in advance so homestays can prepare something for you.
